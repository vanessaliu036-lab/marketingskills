## Purpose

Use this as a structural and typographic reference for restrained, editorial, premium web interfaces.

Do not clone OpenAI branding. Preserve the user's own brand palette, logo, photography, voice, and conversion hierarchy.

## Visual Thesis

- Content-first, editorial composition.
- Strong typography carries hierarchy; decoration is minimal.
- Large areas of whitespace create authority and calm.
- Structure comes from spacing and very light borders rather than shadows or heavy cards.
- One visually dominant action is preferable to many competing CTAs.
- Photography or product imagery should do real visual work rather than act as filler.

## Layout Rules

- Favor contained page widths around 1200px.
- Use asymmetric two-column editorial compositions where useful.
- Avoid dense 3+ column card grids for premium storytelling surfaces.
- Separate sections mainly with whitespace rather than color-block backgrounds.
- Use 32–64px section rhythm for normal content zones, with larger spacing for hero transitions.
- Keep cards visually quiet: no heavy shadows, minimal border radius, minimal chrome.

## Typography Rules

- Use a neutral humanist/geometric sans.
- Recommended substitutes: Inter, Söhne, or system UI when the original font is unavailable.
- Keep headline tracking slightly tight.
- Avoid very heavy weights; 400–600 is enough for most hierarchy.
- Suggested scale: 13 / 16 / 18 / 22 / 28 / 48px.
- Body copy should remain comfortably readable, generally 16–18px with generous leading.

## Interaction Rules

- Primary CTA: one strong filled treatment.
- Secondary actions: outlined or ghost treatment.
- Avoid icon-heavy buttons unless the icon has a functional role.
- Hover effects should be subtle surface or opacity changes.
- Motion should preserve the editorial restraint rather than become decorative spectacle.

## Shape & Surface Rules

- Cards/images: approximately 6px radius.
- Pills are appropriate for compact controls, tags, filters, or small actions.
- Use low-opacity borders instead of hard gray outlines.
- Avoid generic SaaS softness: large 16–24px rounded cards, thick shadows, and floating glass panels.

## Image Strategy

- Prefer one strong, full-bleed image over many small decorative images.
- Use real brand/product/editorial photography when available.
- Let the image define a section; avoid framing it inside excessive UI chrome.
- Do not fake complex photography with CSS shapes.

- black-only CTA logic

- burgundy, cream, coffee-brown palette
- real Cambodian coffee/origin photography
- premium specialty-coffee positioning

## OCC Hero Translation

Recommended OCC interpretation:
- cream or restrained photographic canvas
- large left-aligned headline with tight tracking
- one dominant origin/coffee image occupying 45–60% of the visual field
- a single primary CTA
- secondary metadata kept very small and quiet
- use whitespace and hairline rules instead of decorative panels
- motion limited to subtle image reveal, cursor focus, or restrained text entrance

## Anti-Clone Rule

Use this reference as a design grammar, not a visual copy. Brand-specific color, photography, copy, and information architecture always override the source.
